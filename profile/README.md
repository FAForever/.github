## The FAForever project

### Who we are
[FAForever](http://www.faforever.com) is a community to keep the multiplayer community around our beloved RTS Supreme Commander: Forged Alliance alive.
From a legal perspective we are a registered association in Denmark.

For over a decade now, we develop open source applications & tools that run our community services.

Interested? Join our developer chat at Zulip [here](https://faforever.zulipchat.com).


### About our code

Phew. 130 repositories and still counting? So what do we have here? First of all, try to ignore all the archived ones. These have been superseded and are no longer used in FAF.

We have
* [docs](https://github.com/orgs/FAForever/repositories?q=topic%3Adocs),
* [backend services](https://github.com/orgs/FAForever/repositories?q=topic%3Abackend),
* [clients & tools](https://github.com/orgs/FAForever/repositories?q=topic%3Afrontend),
* [infrastructure as code](https://github.com/orgs/FAForever/repositories?q=topic%3Aiac),
* [featured mods](https://github.com/orgs/FAForever/repositories?q=topic%3Afeatured-mod),
* [regular mods](https://github.com/orgs/FAForever/repositories?q=topic%3Amod) and
* [other cool stuff](https://github.com/orgs/FAForever/repositories?q=-topic%3Adocs%2Cbackend%2Cfrontend%2Ciac%2Cfeatured-mod%2Cmod)
